What the numbers mean

8,979 of 16,276 boards (55.2%) sit on an ATS platform that publishes its own job total, so those fetches are checked against the vendor’s figure board by board, every day. The rest are labelled unverifiable rather than presented as complete — that distinction is the product.

Of the 16,276 boards listed, 15,663 returned at least one open role on the cut date, 498 were reachable and simply had nothing open, and 115 could not be reached at all and say so on their row. The buyer page headlines 15,663 because that is the number with roles behind it; this pack lists all 16,276 because a board with nothing open is still coverage.

Of those 498 empties, 188 were checked against the ATS vendor’s own published total and it was also zero — employers provably hiring nobody on the cut date, not boards that failed to read. Filter boards-completeness.csv to roles_fetched = 0 and verdict = complete to see them.

932,079 posting rows collapse to 854,213 unique roles, a 8.4% dedup collapse across 10 platforms. History runs 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-24, 25 days recorded, forward-only: it is not backfilled and cannot be.

No personal data. The stored schema is company, ATS token, job id, title, location, department, updated date and URL — no candidates, no applicants, no names, no contact details. Every endpoint is the ATS vendor’s own documented public API: no login, no session cookie, no CAPTCHA, no IP-block circumvention.
