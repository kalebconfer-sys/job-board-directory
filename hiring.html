Which companies are adding roles right now, and which are cutting

A job count tells you how many roles a company has open today. It cannot tell you whether that number is climbing or falling, because a count taken once is a photograph. This is the movement: every role that opened and every role that closed across 15,736 company career boards, dated, with the crawler's own artefacts taken out.

What this does not cover

  • This is 8 days of history (2026-09-14 to 2026-09-21). It is a new index, and the window rolls, so it is short by design. A window this length catches a hiring freeze in the week it starts; it cannot tell you a quarterly trend, and this page does not pretend otherwise.
  • 15,736 boards are followed nightly, out of 29,060 in the directory. Being in the directory means the address is known and confirmed live. Only the followed set has an opened/closed history, and only that set appears above.
  • 51,519 role changes are one requisition moving location, not hiring. A board that advertises the same job in three cities and rotates which it shows reads as a close and an open. Those are marked in the data file.
  • 783,999 changes are the index's own coverage moving — a board read for the first time, or one that returned empty. They are excluded from every ranking on this page. Left in, they put a hospital system at the top at +1,496 on the morning its board was first read.
  • 58.1% of closures came from a board whose own published total we could check against. The rest are boards that publish no count.

How a role change gets recorded

  • Every followed board is fetched from the vendor's own public API, once a day.
  • A role present yesterday and absent today is a closure; absent yesterday and present today is an opening.
  • Openings are checked against the employer's own last-modified timestamp, which is the only signal here that does not come from our fetch.
  • Days when the index's own coverage changed are detected and those events are excluded from the rankings rather than sold as hiring.
  • Every figure on this page is read from the measurement at build time. Nothing here is typed in by hand.

Every address here was observed in a public source and then confirmed against the vendor's own public API. Counts are what was seen when the board was last checked and boards change daily, so treat a count as a timestamped observation, not a promise. Nothing here is behind a login and no access control was circumvented.
