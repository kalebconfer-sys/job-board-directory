Which companies are hiring salespeople right now

Every salespeople role that opened and every one that closed across 14,481 company career boards, 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-05. A job count tells you who has the most openings today; this tells you which direction that number is moving.

25,140 of the window's 476,530 role changes were classified sales from the job title. The classifier is ours and will not match anyone else's definition exactly.

Why these are hiring events and not crawler noise. Every opening is checked against the employer's own last-modified timestamp on the posting — the one number here that does not come from our fetch. 52.5% of openings carry a vendor timestamp within two days of the day we called them opened, median lag 1 day.

On its own that would prove nothing, so here is the control: requisitions already live on 2026-10-29, which are new by no definition, sit at 8.4% within two days against the same reference date, median lag 31 days. Openings measured the same way sit at 25.8%. A 3.1× separation.

42.4% of openings carry a vendor date more than a week old and are published as such rather than averaged away.

What this does not cover

  • This is 8 days of history (2026-10-29 to 2026-11-05). It is a new index, and the window rolls, so it is short by design. A window this length catches a hiring freeze in the week it starts; it cannot tell you a quarterly trend, and this page does not pretend otherwise.
  • 14,481 boards are followed nightly, out of 28,617 in the directory. Being in the directory means the address is known and confirmed live. Only the followed set has an opened/closed history, and only that set appears above.
  • 77,068 role changes are one requisition moving location, not hiring. A board that advertises the same job in three cities and rotates which it shows reads as a close and an open. Those are marked in the data file.
  • 135,814 changes are the index's own coverage moving — a board read for the first time, or one that returned empty. They are excluded from every ranking on this page. Left in, they put a hospital system at the top at +1,496 on the morning its board was first read.
  • 36.9% of closures came from a board whose own published total we could check against. The rest are boards that publish no count.
