Which companies are hiring in Paris right now

Every role that opened and every role that closed in Paris across 15,663 company career boards, 2026-09-17 to 2026-09-24. A job count tells you who has the most openings in a place today; this tells you which direction that number is moving.

16.2% of the role changes in this window carry a location string this index recognises (47,879 of 295,982). Employers write that field by hand and the same city arrives in many spellings, so anything that did not match a known place exactly — a suburb, a region, a country on its own — is left out rather than guessed into the nearest city. Why these are hiring events and not crawler noise. Every opening is checked against the employer's own last-modified timestamp on the posting — the one number here that does not come from our fetch. 97.4% of openings carry a vendor timestamp within two days of the day we called them opened, median lag 1 day.

On its own that would prove nothing, so here is the control: requisitions already live on 2026-09-17, which are new by no definition, sit at 7.1% within two days against the same reference date, median lag 34 days. Openings measured the same way sit at 44.5%. A 6.3× separation.

2.0% of openings carry a vendor date more than a week old and are published as such rather than averaged away.

What this does not cover

  • This is 8 days of history (2026-09-17 to 2026-09-24). It is a new index, and the window rolls, so it is short by design. A window this length catches a hiring freeze in the week it starts; it cannot tell you a quarterly trend, and this page does not pretend otherwise.
  • 15,663 boards are followed nightly, out of 29,060 in the directory. Being in the directory means the address is known and confirmed live. Only the followed set has an opened/closed history, and only that set appears above.
  • 84,298 role changes are one requisition moving location, not hiring. A board that advertises the same job in three cities and rotates which it shows reads as a close and an open. Those are marked in the data file.
  • 7,715 changes are the index's own coverage moving — a board read for the first time, or one that returned empty. They are excluded from every ranking on this page. Left in, they put a hospital system at the top at +1,496 on the morning its board was first read.
  • 58.4% of closures came from a board whose own published total we could check against. The rest are boards that publish no count.
