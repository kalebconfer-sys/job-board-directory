How to read this

  • A count is the number of distinct requisitions on the board that night. A night the board could not be read is a gap, not a zero.
  • Workday boards were read to a 2,000-posting cap until 2026-10-05. Such reads show as 2,000+ and are left out of every change figure.
  • Change figures use count-verified boards only. Subsidiary boards are listed but left out of the totals.
  • Not collected: Taleo, and iCIMS portals whose robots.txt bars crawlers. 2 Greenhouse boards without a published employer name are left out. A company missing here is not a company without openings.
  • The full index behind this page covers every board on the same platforms, nightly. For a licence or a custom cut: kaleb@revivacatch.com.

Every address here was observed in a public source and then confirmed against the vendor's own public API. Counts are what was seen when the board was last checked and boards change daily, so treat a count as a timestamped observation, not a promise. Nothing here is behind a login and no access control was circumvented.

Questions, corrections, or a board that should be on this list: kaleb@revivacatch.com.
