Every designers role that opened and every one that closed across 1,200 company career boards, 2026-09-02 to 2026-09-09. A job count tells you who has the most openings today; this tells you which direction that number is moving.

Why these are hiring events and not crawler noise. Every opening is checked against the employer's own last-modified timestamp on the posting — the one number here that does not come from our fetch. 58.3% of openings carry a vendor timestamp within two days of the day we called them opened, median lag 1 day.

On its own that would prove nothing, so here is the control: requisitions already live on 2026-09-02, which are new by no definition, sit at 6.0% within two days against the same reference date, median lag 22 days. Openings measured the same way sit at 21.5%. A 3.6× separation.

38.9% of openings carry a vendor date more than a week old and are published as such rather than averaged away.

What this does not cover

  • This is 8 days of history (2026-09-02 to 2026-09-09). It is a new index, and the window rolls, so it is short by design. A window this length catches a hiring freeze in the week it starts; it cannot tell you a quarterly trend, and this page does not pretend otherwise.
  • 1,200 boards are followed nightly, out of 25,568 in the directory. Being in the directory means the address is known and confirmed live. Only the followed set has an opened/closed history, and only that set appears above.
  • 2,106 role changes are one requisition moving location, not hiring. A board that advertises the same job in three cities and rotates which it shows reads as a close and an open. Those are marked in the data file.
  • 5,754 changes are the index's own coverage moving — a board read for the first time, or one that returned empty. They are excluded from every ranking on this page. Left in, they put a hospital system at the top at +1,496 on the morning its board was first read.
  • 45.8% of closures came from a board whose own published total we could check against. The rest are boards that publish no count.
