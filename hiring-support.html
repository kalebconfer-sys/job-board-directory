What this does not cover

  • This is 8 days of history (2026-09-01 to 2026-09-08). It is a new index, and the window rolls, so it is short by design. A window this length catches a hiring freeze in the week it starts; it cannot tell you a quarterly trend, and this page does not pretend otherwise.
  • 1,193 boards are followed nightly, out of 25,568 in the directory. Being in the directory means the address is known and confirmed live. Only the followed set has an opened/closed history, and only that set appears above.
  • 2,051 role changes are one requisition moving location, not hiring. A board that advertises the same job in three cities and rotates which it shows reads as a close and an open. Those are marked in the data file.
  • 5,285 changes are the index's own coverage moving — a board read for the first time, or one that returned empty. They are excluded from every ranking on this page. Left in, they put a hospital system at the top at +1,496 on the morning its board was first read.
  • 48.7% of closures came from a board whose own published total we could check against. The rest are boards that publish no count.
