Every customer support staff role that opened and every one that closed across 15,641 company career boards, 2026-09-18 to 2026-09-25. A job count tells you who has the most openings today; this tells you which direction that number is moving.

Why these are hiring events and not crawler noise. Every opening is checked against the employer's own last-modified timestamp on the posting — the one number here that does not come from our fetch. 97.4% of openings carry a vendor timestamp within two days of the day we called them opened, median lag 1 day.

On its own that would prove nothing, so here is the control: requisitions already live on 2026-09-18, which are new by no definition, sit at 7.3% within two days against the same reference date, median lag 32 days. Openings measured the same way sit at 44.3%. A 6.0× separation.

2.1% of openings carry a vendor date more than a week old and are published as such rather than averaged away.

What this does not cover

  • This is 8 days of history (2026-09-18 to 2026-09-25). It is a new index, and the window rolls, so it is short by design. A window this length catches a hiring freeze in the week it starts; it cannot tell you a quarterly trend, and this page does not pretend otherwise.
  • 15,641 boards are followed nightly, out of 29,060 in the directory. Being in the directory means the address is known and confirmed live. Only the followed set has an opened/closed history, and only that set appears above.
  • 82,557 role changes are one requisition moving location, not hiring. A board that advertises the same job in three cities and rotates which it shows reads as a close and an open. Those are marked in the data file.
  • 10,019 changes are the index's own coverage moving — a board read for the first time, or one that returned empty. They are excluded from every ranking on this page. Left in, they put a hospital system at the top at +1,496 on the morning its board was first read.
  • 58.7% of closures came from a board whose own published total we could check against. The rest are boards that publish no count.
